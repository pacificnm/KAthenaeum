Setting Up the AI Assistant

Reading, searching and maps work without anything else. Asking questions needs Ollama, which runs language models on your computer. It is not part of Debian: install it from ollama.com, then download the two models KAthenaeum uses by default: 

ollama pull qwen3.5:2b
ollama pull nomic-embed-text

qwen3.5:2b writes the answers; nomic-embed-text finds the passages that match a question and links the answer's sentences to their sources. Both were chosen by measuring them on a laptop: they run on a processor or an integrated graphics chip. Settings → Models shows whether Ollama is running and has them, and lets you choose others.
