12.1.

Asking a question says Ollama cannot be reached.

Start Ollama (on most systems, systemctl start ollama), then press Check Again in Settings. Reading and searching work without it.

12.2.

A model is missing.

Settings shows the command to download it, for example ollama pull qwen3.5:2b.

12.3.

The answer says it could not find anything, although the library has an article on it.

Questions find articles by their words. Name the subject as the article's title does (“What is the boiling point of ethanol?”), or open the article and ask about it there with Ask.

12.4.

A library is marked as not Wikipedia-style.

KAthenaeum can read and search only libraries made the way Kiwix makes Wikipedia's. Others (such as Stack Exchange or TED) may open, but their articles cannot be searched or used for answers.

12.5.

The map is empty.

Download the world overview and a region in Settings → Maps. The map shows only what has been downloaded.

12.6.

Clicking a place shows no article.

Not every place has a Wikipedia article, and the article must be in one of your libraries: a small topic library will not have it. Search the Libraries searches for the place's name instead.
