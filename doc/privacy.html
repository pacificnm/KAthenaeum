Chapter 11. Privacy and the Network

KAthenaeum works offline. What you read, search for, ask and write stays on your computer: questions go to Ollama on this computer, never to a service on the Internet, and articles are shown with nothing loaded from the web. It connects to the Internet only:

  • to the Kiwix catalog and its downloads, when you open the Library Manager, download a library, or check for library updates (by default once a week);

  • to Protomaps and Geofabrik, when you add or update a map region.

Links to web sites in articles open in your browser, only when you click them.
