Chapter 6. Research Projects

A project gathers what you find on one subject. Choose Projects, then New Project…, and give it a title and, if you like, a goal: the question you are researching. A project has three views: Sources, Chat and Documents.

Sources and Notes

Add to a project:

  • an article: Add to Project… in its header, or Add Article… in the project;

  • a passage: select it in an article, right-click, Save to Project…;

  • an answer: Save to Project… under it;

  • a whole chat: Add to Project… in Chats.

Each source can have a note. Notes appear beside the source, on the passage in its article, and in your documents' preview.
