KAthenaeum is a private, offline Wikipedia library with an AI research assistant. It reads Kiwix ZIM files, searches them, answers questions from them with a small language model running on your own computer, keeps research projects with your notes and documents, and shows offline maps whose places open their Wikipedia articles.
