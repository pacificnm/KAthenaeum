Documents

New Document creates a Markdown document. It opens in a window of its own with Kate's editor (highlighting, spell checking, search and replace, and everything you can set in Configure Editor…) beside a live preview. The document is saved as you type.

Cite inserts a link to one of the project's articles, or quotes a saved passage with its source. Links in the preview open the article in the main window. Export… saves the document as a Markdown file, with a reference list in place of the links (which mean nothing outside KAthenaeum); Export… in the project's header exports the whole project.
