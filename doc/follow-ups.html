Follow-up Questions and Chats

Ask a follow-up in the same chat (“and how deep is it?”): the assistant knows what “it” refers to. New Chat starts afresh.

Every chat is saved with its answers, sources and citations. Chats lists them, newest first: open one to read it or continue it, or Rename, Delete or Add to Project… it. A chat about an article, or one held in a project, is marked as such.
