Drafts Written by the AI

Draft with AI writes a new document for you to edit:

Write From Your Instructions…

Give a title and say what the document should cover and how, in as much detail as you like: “A full report on how Crater Lake formed. Start with Mount Mazama and the eruption, then the caldera and how the lake filled. Formal tone, with a short conclusion.” Choose a short document (about three sections) or a full report (up to six longer ones), and whether it may use the whole library. The model plans the sections from your instructions, then writes each one from the sources that fit it. KAthenaeum adds the source numbers, [?] on what to check, and a reference list. A full report takes a few minutes.

Summary of the Project

A summary of each of the project's articles (up to fifteen) and an overview of what they say about its goal.

Notes from a chat

The questions explored, the findings and the open questions of one of the project's chats.

Stop ends a draft and keeps nothing. Drafts come from a small model: read them against their sources.
