Chapter 10. Keyboard Shortcuts

KeysAction
CtrlFGo to the search box in the sidebar
F1Open this handbook
EnterAsk the question, or search for the text, that you have typed
+ / −Zoom the map in or out
EscClose the place shown on the map
CtrlShiftPShow or hide the preview in a document's window

A document's window has all of Kate's editing shortcuts.
